AI Basics — Understand the Foundations

Learn the core ideas behind modern AI: key concepts, simple models, and how AI systems are used in real products. Built for absolute beginners.

What you’ll learn

  • Explain the difference between AI, ML and deep learning in simple terms.
  • Read a confusion matrix, precision/recall and accuracy like a pro.
  • Understand datasets, training vs validation, and common pitfalls.
  • Recognize where AI fits in real products and what data it needs.

Curriculum

Module 1 — Intro to AI 12 min

What AI is (and isn’t), quick history, examples you already use daily.

Module 2 — Data & Models 24 min

Datasets, features, labels. Training, validation, overfitting, underfitting. Simple classification example.

Module 3 — Evaluating AI 18 min

Accuracy vs precision/recall, confusion matrix, ROC/PR curves (high level, visual intuition).

Module 4 — Real-world Use 20 min

Where AI helps in apps, data needs, ethics and privacy, when *not* to use AI.
